Unofficial fan analysis, not affiliated with or endorsed by Sony Pictures Networks India or the show's producers. It contains only facts about the deals and our own analysis; no video, images or transcripts. The data was compiled from public sources (Wikipedia season pages, cross-checked against fan archives and news reports) and may contain errors. If you spot one, please report it.

Valuations are implied from the equity cheque: amount ÷ equity %. Deals made mostly of debt, and asks below ₹1 lakh, are left out of the valuation figures.
